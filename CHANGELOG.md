# Changelog

## 22 November 2026

New
- Away from home. The app, widgets and new-device alerts now work when you're out, through a VPN to your home network (Tailscale or ZeroTier). A new page, More, Settings, VPN, Away from home, explains the setup and tests the connection.
- A banner on every tab shows when you're connected away from home.

Improved
- The app reconnects by itself when your phone moves between home Wi-Fi and a VPN.
- Clearer messages when the router can't be reached, saying whether to join the Wi-Fi or turn on the VPN.

## 15 November 2026

New
- Favourites. Open a device and tap the star to keep it at the top of the Devices list. Favourites also get their own section on Home, showing whether each one is online, and a Favourites filter on Devices.
- Device history. The app now remembers when each device was last online, so offline devices show "Last online 3 hours ago" instead of just "offline". Each device also shows the date the app first noticed it. This is stored on your phone only and starts from this update.

Improved
- Tapping a device on Home (Favourites or Top clients) now opens that device directly.
- Tap an IP or MAC address in a device's details to copy it.
- The Devices list remembers the sort order you chose.
- The exported device list includes favourite and last online columns.
- Screen readers now announce switches and tick boxes properly, with their on or off state.
